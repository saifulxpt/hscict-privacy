Official Policy

Privacy Policy for HSC ICT+

Application: HSC ICT+ (com.neurobyte.hsc_ict_plus)
Developer: Neurobyte (neurobyteit.com)
Last Updated: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Neurobyte develops HSC ICT+ to provide Bangladeshi Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) students with interactive ICT learning, MCQ practice, board CQ suggestions, logic gate visualizers, and real-time 1v1 challenges. We are committed to safeguarding student privacy and maintaining transparency in data practices.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Students can use almost all core study materials in HSC ICT+ without logging in or providing personal data. We only collect data when optional cloud-powered features are used:

  • Google Sign-In: If you use Google Authentication, we collect your display name and email address to authenticate your profile.
  • Student Profile (Optional): You may optionally provide your phone number, college name, and district.
  • Communication Consent: If you check the optional consent box, we may send educational notices or course information via WhatsApp. You can revoke this anytime.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Anonymous device tokens are used solely for match notifications in ICT Battle and study reminders.
Zero Advertising Policy: HSC ICT+ does not track your device with Google Advertising ID (AD_ID). We do not include any third-party ad networks, trackers, or commercial data brokers.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

All cloud features use Google Firebase infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services — Application distribution and security verification.
  • Firebase Authentication — Secure token-based user sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore — Real-time multiplayer rooms and student profile storage.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — Push notification alerts.

All network traffic is encrypted over HTTPS / TLS.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Under Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users have full control to permanently delete their account and associated personal data:

How to Delete Your Account and Data:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the HSC ICT+ app → Go to Student Profile → Tap "প্রোফাইল মুছে ফেলুন" (Delete Account) → Confirm. All your Firebase records and profile data are wiped immediately.
  2. Web-Based Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app, visit our Data Deletion Request Page or send an email to support@neurobyteit.com with the subject "HSC ICT+ Account Deletion". Your records will be deleted within 48 to 72 hours.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for students in grades 11 and 12 (typically 16–19 years of age). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If any parent or guardian becomes aware that their child under 13 provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Independent Educational Disclaimer

HSC ICT+ is an independent digital study aid created by Neurobyte. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with the National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB) or the Ministry of Education, Bangladesh.

HSC ICT+ Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: HSC ICT+ (com.neurobyte.hsc_ict_plus)
Developer: Neurobyte
Policy URL: Privacy Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account and Data Deletion Policy, users of HSC ICT+ have the full right to delete their student profile, account credentials, and all associated personal data stored on our servers.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the app installed:
  1. Open the HSC ICT+ mobile app on your Android device.
  2. From the Dashboard or Drawer menu, open Student Profile (শিক্ষার্থী প্রোফাইল).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "প্রোফাইল মুছে ফেলুন" (Delete Account & Data).
  4. Confirm the deletion modal.

✓ Confirm your Google account again. The app reports success after deleting your saved profile, linked Battle invitation registration, and Firebase account.

Option 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (Without Installing App)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access:

You can request complete deletion of your account and personal data by sending an email from the Google email address used to log into the app:

  • Recipient: support@neurobyteit.com or saiful@neurobyteit.com
  • Subject: HSC ICT+ Data Deletion Request
  • Information to provide: Your registered Google Account email address.
Send Deletion Request Email

Our support team will process your request and permanently remove all data from Firebase within 48 to 72 hours. You will receive a confirmation email upon completion.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Your Firebase Authentication User Record (email, UID, sign-in tokens).
  • Your Student Profile record in Cloud Firestore (name, phone number, college, district, marketing preferences).
  • Your Push Notification Token on Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Your 1v1 match history records.

HSC ICT+ Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: HSC ICT+ (com.neurobyte.hsc_ict_plus)
Developer: Neurobyte
Policy URL: Privacy Policy

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open HSC ICT+ and go to Student Profile (শিক্ষার্থী প্রোফাইল).
  2. Select the account deletion option and confirm.
  3. Verify the same Google account you use to sign in and wait for the confirmation in the app.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@neurobyteit.com with the subject HSC ICT+ Account Deletion and the Google email address used to sign in. If you have lost access to that email account, contact support for help verifying ownership. Never send your password or a verification code.

Send Deletion Request

Support will verify the request and confirm when the account deletion is complete.

What is removed

  • The HSC ICT+ Firebase Authentication record and saved student profile, including the name, phone number, college, district and optional uploaded photo.
  • Any linked legacy Battle invitation registration.
  • When deletion is completed in the app, the saved profile cache and Battle history on that device.

Question reports are handled separately. Ask support to remove personal information from reports you submitted. Local study notes and practice activity can be removed by clearing HSC ICT+ storage on your device. See the Privacy Policy for how temporary Battle rooms and app notifications are handled.

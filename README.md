## 1. Overview
HSC ICT+ is an educational mobile application built by Neurobyte to help Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) students in Bangladesh study Information & Communication Technology (ICT). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how you can control or delete your data.

We prioritize student privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Authentication & Profile Information (Optional)
Students can use the core learning features of HSC ICT+ (such as Chapter notes, suggestions, MCQ practice, offline PDF readers, logic gate visualizers, and base converter) **without creating an account**.

If you choose to participate in **ICT Battle** (real-time 1v1 quiz with friends) or choose to create a student profile:
- **Google Sign-In:** We receive your name and email address through Google Firebase Authentication to verify your identity.
- **Student Profile (Optional):** You may optionally provide your College Name, District, and Phone Number.
- **WhatsApp Notification Consent (Optional):** Only if you explicitly check the opt-in checkbox, we may use your phone number to send educational updates or course news via WhatsApp. You can revoke this consent at any time.

### B. Device Identifiers & Push Notifications
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token:** We store an anonymous device push token to send 1v1 challenge invites and daily study reminders. You can disable notifications anytime in the app settings or system settings.

### C. Data We DO NOT Collect
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not request or track GPS or precise network location.
- **No Device Camera or Microphone Access:** The app does not access your camera, audio, or media storage.
- **No Advertising Identifier (AD_ID):** We do not track users with Google Advertising ID. There are no third-party ad networks (e.g. AdMob) in the app.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use reputable, industry-standard infrastructure from Google:
- **Google Play Services:** App distribution and core Android platform services.
- **Firebase Authentication:** Secure Google Sign-In verification.
- **Cloud Firestore:** Cloud database for real-time 1v1 ICT Battle rooms and optional student profile storage.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** Sending match alerts and study notifications.

All transmission of data between the app and our services is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).

## 4. Account and Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliant)

We respect your right to completely remove your data at any time. We provide two ways to delete your account and associated data:

### Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion
1. Open the **HSC ICT+** app.
2. Open the **Student Profile** screen from the drawer or dashboard.
3. Tap **"প্রোফাইল মুছে ফেলুন" (Delete Account)** at the bottom.
4. Confirm the prompt.
5. Your Firestore profile record, FCM registration, and Firebase Authentication account are immediately and permanently erased from the server.

### Method 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (Without Installing App)
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request full data deletion via web or email:
- **Email Request:** Send an email from your registered Google account to **`support@neurobyteit.com`** or **`saiful@neurobyteit.com`** with the subject: `HSC ICT+ Data Deletion Request`.
- **Processing Time:** All personal data associated with your email and user UID will be permanently purged from our Firebase Auth and Firestore records within **48 to 72 hours**.
- **Web Portal:** [https://saifulxpt.github.io/HSCICT/delete-account.html](https://saifulxpt.github.io/HSCICT/delete-account.html)

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)
HSC ICT+ is intended for Higher Secondary students (grades 11, 12, and college candidates), generally aged **16 years and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a user under 13 has provided personal data, we promptly delete it from our servers.
